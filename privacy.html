Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

1. What data we collect

When you use the WearWise style advisor, you optionally provide: a photo, height, weight, body shape, skin tone, hair details, occasion and style preferences. If you upgrade to Pro, we collect your name, email address and payment information (processed securely by Stripe).

2. How your photo is used

Your photo is sent directly to Anthropic's Claude AI API to analyse visible features (skin tone, face shape, hair, proportions) for styling advice. Your photo is never stored on our servers. It exists only in your browser session and is discarded immediately after the API response. We do not retain, store, or share your photo with any third party other than the Anthropic API for processing.

3. Affiliate links

Some shopping links on WearWise are affiliate links. This means if you click a link and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This never affects which products or stores we recommend — our advice is always based on what suits you best.

4. Payments

All payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment processing: stripe.com/privacy.

5. Cookies & local storage

We use browser local storage to remember your free report count and Pro subscription status. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.
